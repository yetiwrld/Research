# Sales Playbook — Call Openers, Objection Handling & Sequencing

Companion to `top-prospects.md` (ranked leads) and `outreach-drafts.md` (first-touch emails/DMs).
Rules: only use verified facts from the database; never invent numbers, names, or claims.
If a fact isn't in the lead record, don't say it — say "from what I can see online."

---

## 1. Call openers (30 seconds, permission-based)

**Expired/dead website (Ken Cooks, Kaycie Kyle, Moonlight, Osborne)**
> "Hi, it's [Name] — I help local [restaurants/salons/garages] turn reviews into bookings.
> I noticed your site [is down / the domain expired] while your [800+ Google ratings / 987 Fresha reviews]
> are still sending people looking for you. Is that something you've already got handled,
> or worth 10 minutes?"

**Platform-dependent, no owned site (Flower ATL, Paw Hub, Remedy, Shawarma's King, 786)**
> "Hi, it's [Name] — quick observation: you've got [120 Booksy reviews / 1,687 Google ratings]
> but no website of your own, so all of that demand lands on [Booksy's / a directory's] page.
> I build [booking / ordering] websites for independents like you. Open to a 10-minute look?"

**Broken/weak site (Nail Care, Sakushi, Goat in the Tree, Nubreed, Streatery)**
> "Hi, it's [Name] — love what you're doing; [365 Yelp reviews / fully-booked weekends] says a lot.
> I did notice [clients can't book online / the online booking flow seems broken / the site doesn't take orders].
> That's usually a quick fix with a big payoff. Worth a short chat?"

**Voicemail (any lead, <20 seconds)**
> "Hi, it's [Name] — I spotted something specific on your web presence costing you [bookings/orders],
> happy to share it free either way. My number is [___]. I'll also send it by [text/email] so you have it in writing."

## 2. Discovery questions (after permission)

1. "When someone Googles you today, where do they end up — and can they [book/order] there?"
2. "How are [pickup orders / appointments / catering enquiries] coming in right now — phone, DM, apps?"
3. "Roughly what share of orders goes through delivery/booking platforms vs direct?"
4. "Anything customers complain they can't find or do online?"
5. "If the site handled [booking/ordering] properly, what would that free up for you each week?"

## 3. Objection handling

**"We get all our business from word of mouth / we're fully booked."**
> "That's exactly why I'm calling — [Kaycie Kyle-style: booked 3 weeks out] means every person who can't
> reach you goes to a competitor. The site isn't about more demand, it's about catching the demand
> you're already turning away — waitlists, deposits, gift vouchers, retail."

**"We already have Facebook/Instagram."**
> "Social is great for discovery, but it can't take [a booking with a deposit / a pickup order at midnight /
> a catering quote]. Your [653 reviews / 1,150 TripAdvisor reviews] deserve a home you own that converts
> followers into revenue while you sleep."

**"We tried a website before, it didn't work."**
> "Most [restaurant/salon] sites are brochures — pretty but no booking, no ordering, no reason to return.
> What I build is a [booking/ordering] tool first: [online scheduling + deposits / direct pickup ordering /
> catering quotes]. Different thing entirely. Can I show you what that looks like for a [salon/shawarma shop]?"

**"How much?" (too early)**
> "Depends on whether it's a fix or a rebuild — [Sakushi-style fixes / Osborne-style rebuilds] are very different
> jobs. The 10-minute call is just to see which one yours is, then I'll give you a fixed quote. Fair?"

**"Just send me something."**
> Send the relevant outreach draft + one screenshot of the specific gap (dead domain page, missing booking button,
> broken image). Then: "Sent — the screenshot shows exactly what customers hit. I'll follow up [day]; if it's
> not a fit, tell me and I'll close the loop."

**"We're too small / it's just me." (Nubreed-style solo operators)**
> "Solo is exactly who this pays off fastest — every phone-tag booking is time you're not earning.
> Online scheduling with deposits fills your route without you touching the phone."

## 4. Outreach sequencing (per lead)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | First touch: call if phone verified, else email/DM draft from `outreach-drafts.md` |
| 2 | If no reply: second channel (e.g., called → now Instagram DM with the screenshot) |
| 5 | Follow-up message (template at the end of `outreach-drafts.md`) |
| 12 | Break-up: "Should I close this out? Happy to — just didn't want [expired domain / broken booking] sitting there costing you." |
| — | Stop after break-up unless they re-engage. Never pitch C leads until re-verified. |

**Prioritisation:** A leads first (Ken Cooks, Paw Hub, Kaycie Kyle, Flower ATL, Remedy, Shawarma's King, Osborne, Moonlight, 786),
then high-demand B's (Maureen's, Simone's, Diamond, Nail Care, Sakushi, Goat, Nubreed). Verify Kaycie Kyle's trading name
and Moonlight's site status same-day before calling (flags in `top-prospects.md`).
