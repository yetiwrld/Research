## Operating principles applied

- Every A/B lead verified by **3+ independent public signals** (website, reviews, social, press, directories).
- No invented facts: unverifiable fields are marked **"Not verified"**.
- No revenue/affordability claims — commercial fit is evidence-based only.
- Only **public business contact information** collected.
- Closed businesses, major chains, and excellent-website businesses are rejected with reasons, not silently dropped.
